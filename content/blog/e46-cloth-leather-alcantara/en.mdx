Ask an E46 owner what their seats are and you'll hear "cloth", "leather" or "Alcantara". BMW didn't sell them that way. It sold upholsteries by name: Jacquard, Tex 2000, Gran Turismo, Laser, Montana, Nappa. Each name means a particular material, on particular seats, in particular bodies and years. Knowing the names is the quickest way to read a seat listing correctly.

## Cloth: what came as standard

BMW's German price lists name the standard cloth per body. [S1, HIGH]

| Body | Standard cloth in the lists we hold |
| --- | --- |
| Sedan, Touring | Jacquard (from the 2002 lists on) |
| Coupé, Convertible | Tex 2000 |
| Compact | flat-weave cloth on every model except the 325ti, which came with sport seats |

From BMW's German price lists, 2002 to 2006. [S1, HIGH]

Before 2002 the picture is thinner. The September 1998 Sedan list names a flock cloth, and the September 2000 Convertible list a cloth called "Lifestyle", but both are poor scans. [S1, LOW] BMW's parts catalogue, as read on our mirror, lists a flock cloth called "Raute" for the Sedan, Touring and Coupé up to September 2001. [S3, MEDIUM]

## Cloth and leather together

Many E46s left the factory in a mix: cloth centre panels with leather bolsters, or the other way round. BMW's German price lists offer these by name. [S1, HIGH]
- **Sedan and Touring:** Gran Turismo, Maglia and Bianco Nero in 2002; Scritto from 2004, and only with the Edition Lifestyle.
- **Coupé:** Gran Turismo, Maglia and Bianco Nero in 2002; Finolino from September 2004.
- **Convertible:** Gran Turismo in March 2004; Finolino from September 2004.
- **Compact:** California in 2002; Monza in 2004.

The Compact had its own range throughout. BMW's catalogue, as read on our mirror, lists Monza, California and Europe for it, and no Gran Turismo. [S3, MEDIUM]

## Alcantara: two different things

"Alcantara" in an E46 listing usually means one of two very different covers.

**"Laser", cloth with Alcantara.** When BMW launched its M Sport package for the 3 Series in May 2001, it described the sport seats as being in cloth "Laser"/Alcantara. [S2, HIGH] BMW's catalogue, as read on our mirror, lists Laser on the sport seats of every E46 body except the M3. [S3, MEDIUM] So most "Alcantara" E46 sport seats you'll see for sale are Laser: cloth with Alcantara, not all-Alcantara.

<Figure src="/blog/e46-cloth-leather-alcantara/laser.webp" alt="AI image of an E46 interior with anthracite cloth and Alcantara Sport front seats, seen from the rear passenger side" width={1200} height={675} caption="Cloth with Alcantara, the 'Laser' look of the M Sport package's seats." credit="AI-generated illustration" />

**BMW Individual Alcantara.** BMW's September 2004 Touring price list also offers a full Alcantara upholstery from BMW Individual, and only with sport seats. [S1, HIGH] That's a different, rarer cover from Laser.

The M3 is its own case. BMW's catalogue, as read on our mirror, lists a leather with Alcantara for the M3 alongside its leathers. [S3, MEDIUM]

## Leather

BMW's German price lists name the leathers. [S1, HIGH]
- **Montana**, the common E46 leather, on every body except the M3.
- **Nappa** ("Exklusivleder Nappa") on the Sedan, Touring and Coupé.
- **Futura** ("Exclusivleder Futura") on the Convertible, in September 2004.
- **The M3:** the Coupé came with a cloth/Nappa leather mix as standard, the Convertible with Nappa leather, and full leather was an option.

In the September 2004 Touring list, the Montana and Nappa leathers came with seat heating included. [S1, HIGH] What they cost is in [What your E46's seats cost new](/en/blog/e46-seats-cost-new).

BMW's catalogue, as read on our mirror, lists further leathers for the Sedan and Touring: High, a perforated Mosaico up to September 2001, and Chameleon from March 2003. [S3, MEDIUM]

## Two dates that change the list

The catalogue's dates show two turning points for the Sedan, Touring and Coupé, as read on our mirror. [S3, MEDIUM]
- **September 2001, the facelift:** the Raute flock cloth, the Mosaico leather and a cloth/leather Gran Turismo in deep green are listed only up to here.
- **March 2003:** the Chameleon leather and the Scritto cloth/leather appear.

A cover name that the catalogue only lists after the facelift, on a seat said to come from a 2000 car, is worth a question.

## Reading the cover in a listing

- **Imitation leather isn't leather.** BMW's catalogue lists imitation leather and vinyl as covers of their own. [S3, MEDIUM] "Kunstleder", "Skai" or "leatherette" in a listing means one of those, not Montana or Nappa.
- **"Alcantara" usually means Laser.** Ask whether it's cloth with Alcantara (Laser) or BMW Individual's full Alcantara, and look at the photos.
- **Alcantara on basic seats is a question.** BMW's Individual Alcantara came only with sport seats. [S1, HIGH]
- **The cover name beats the word "leather".** "Montana" or "Nappa" tells you more than "leather seats".
- **The seat matters as much as the cover.** Whether a set is sport or basic, electric or manual, and which body it's from is in [Sport, electric or ALPINA? How to read an E46 Sedan or Touring seat listing](/en/blog/e46-sedan-touring-seat-listings); the rear seats are in [There is no rear-bench part number either](/en/blog/e46-rear-seats-which-fit).

## Notes on the evidence

**Confidence.** Standard cloths, the named upholsteries, leather with heating and Individual Alcantara with sport seats come from BMW's German price lists (HIGH), except where the 1998 and 2000 scans are marked LOW. Laser on the M Sport package's sport seats comes from BMW's May 2001 press release (HIGH). Which covers each body's seats are listed with, and their dates, come from one mirror of BMW's parts catalogue, read for one model per body (MEDIUM).

**Not established.** Which upholstery was standard or optional on a Sedan, Touring or Coupé from 1999 to 2001: there is no German price list for those years. The catalogue shows which covers exist for a body, not what any one car had. Other markets are not covered; the price lists are German.
