The E46 wasn't facelifted in one go. BMW changed the Sedan and Touring in September 2001, the Coupé and Convertible in March 2003, gave the Compact only a new rear, and left the M3's face almost alone. If you're buying headlights, bumpers or tail lights, which side of that line your car is on matters more than its engine.

## Sedan and Touring: September 2001

BMW's own parts catalogue, as read on our mirrors, starts the facelift headlights and bumpers of the Sedan and Touring from September 2001. [S2, HIGH] BMW presented the result in its press kit for model year 2002, dated 26 October 2001. [S1, HIGH] It lists the changes itself: [S1, HIGH]

- **new headlights**, curved, with the indicators behind clear glass;
- **round fog lamps** in place of the rectangular ones;
- **a wider kidney grille**;
- **a new bonnet and front wings**: the bonnet's power dome now runs up to the A-pillars, the old line above the wheel arches is gone, the wings get a new swage line, and teardrop-shaped side repeaters sit higher on the wing;
- **a new front air dam** on both;
- **a new rear valance and a re-arranged tail light on the Sedan only**: the light's outer shape is unchanged, but the brake light now sits at the top. The Touring got the new front only.

BMW's British press kit adds that BMW "would not call it a facelift", since the 1998 3 Series was "hardly old enough to have suffered from facial ageing". [S1, HIGH] Owners call it one anyway.

Under the skin, the same press kit announces two new four-cylinder engines and new options, among them bi-xenon headlights and a wide-screen navigation monitor. [S1, HIGH]

## Coupé and Convertible: their own facelift, March 2003

The Coupé and Convertible kept their first face in 2001. Their facelift came in March 2003: in BMW's parts catalogue, on two mirrors, every pre-facelift front-end part ends in March 2003 and every facelift part starts then, the same for Coupé and Convertible, in Europe and the US. [S3, HIGH] What changes, by the catalogue: [S3, HIGH]
- the headlights, now with the indicator built in;
- the kidney grille and the bonnet;
- the front bumper;
- the front indicators and the side repeaters;
- at the back, a conversion kit for the rear lights is titled "Facelift 2003".

One thing did change on the Coupé and Convertible in September 2001: in the catalogue, their xenon headlight gives way to bi-xenon then, without changing the car's look. [S3, HIGH] That's why a 2002 Coupé can have bi-xenon and still be "pre-facelift".

## The Compact: only the rear, March 2003

The Compact had its own front from the start. BMW's parts catalogue, as read on our mirror, shows no date split for its headlights. [S4, MEDIUM] What did change, in March 2003, were its rear lights: clear glass up to then, fully red after. [S4, MEDIUM]

## The M3: its own face throughout

The M3 kept its own headlights and grille through all of this: BMW's catalogue, as read on our mirrors, shows no facelift front for it, only the change from xenon to bi-xenon in September 2001. [S3, HIGH]

## Is your car before or after?

The build month decides, not the model year or the registration. The first registration can come months after the car was built, so a Sedan registered in early 2002 can still be a pre-facelift car. [S5] On the car itself, the Sedan and Touring are easiest to tell by the front: curved headlights with clear-glass indicators, round fog lamps and the teardrop side repeaters are facelift. [S1, HIGH]

## What it means for parts

- **Headlights:** the facelift changes the shape of the headlight, so pre- and post-facelift units are different parts. [S2, HIGH] The details are in [Two dates decide every E46 headlight, and 2001 is the trap](/en/blog/e46-headlight-changeovers).
- **Bumpers:** the front bumper changes with each body's facelift, and on the Sedan the rear too. [S1, HIGH] [S2, HIGH] [S3, HIGH] More in [Forget the engine: what really decides which bumper fits your E46](/en/blog/e46-bumpers-body-era-package).
- **The Compact:** its front is its own. See [Same badge, different nose](/en/blog/e46-compact-front-end-bumpers) and [The loner](/en/blog/e46-compact-headlights).

E46BUILD asks only what the fit depends on. For a car registered close to a changeover, such as a 2002 Sedan, it asks which headlights your car has. [S5]

## Notes on the evidence

**Confidence.** What changed on the Sedan and Touring comes from BMW's own press kit of October 2001 (HIGH). The September 2001 and March 2003 changeovers come from BMW's parts catalogue on mirrors with month views (HIGH). The Compact's rear lights and unchanged headlights come from one mirror (MEDIUM).

**Not established.** Whether the M3's side repeaters changed in March 2003; the catalogue shows both versions. What else changed on the Coupé and Convertible in March 2003 beyond the front end and rear lights.
