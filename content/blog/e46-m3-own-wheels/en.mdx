The E46 M3 doesn't share its wheels with the rest of the range. BMW gave it its own styles, its own offsets and, on the Competition and CSL, bigger brakes behind them. This is every wheel BMW listed for the E46 M3, from BMW's own wheel and tyre sheets, price lists and press releases.

## The four summer wheels

| Wheel | Size, front / rear | Construction | Tyres (front / rear) | How it came |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Style 67M, 18-inch** ("M double spoke 67") | 8×18 ET47 / 9×18 ET26 | cast | 225/45 ZR18 / 255/40 ZR18 | standard on the M3 Coupé and Convertible |
| **Style 67M, 19-inch** | 8×19 ET47 / 9.5×19 ET27 | forged and polished | 225/40 ZR19 / 255/35 ZR19 | factory option 792 |
| **Style 163M** ("M cross spoke 163", "M3 CSL design") | 8×19 ET47 / 9.5×19 ET27 | cast | 225/40 R19 / 255/35 R19 | part of the Competition package, from December 2004 |
| **The CSL's own wheel** | 8.5×19 ET44 / 9.5×19 ET27 | not in our sources | 235/35 ZR19 / 265/30 ZR19, Michelin Pilot Sport Cup | standard on the M3 CSL |

From BMW's wheel and tyre sheets, price lists and press releases. [S1, HIGH] [S2, HIGH] [S3, HIGH] [S4, HIGH]

**The 18-inch Style 67M** is the M3's everyday wheel: standard on the Coupé and the Convertible. [S1, HIGH] [S2, HIGH] BMW's parts catalogue, as read on our mirror, calls its finish "chromshadow" and doesn't list it for the CSL. [S5, MEDIUM]

**The 19-inch Style 67M** was the option to tick: number 792, polished and forged, at €1,500 in BMW's German price list of September 2002 and €1,600 from March 2003 to March 2006. [S2, HIGH] BMW's US press kit for the CSL says CSL buyers who often drive on wet roads could opt for these forged 19-inch wheels from the standard M3. [S4, HIGH]

**The Style 163M** came with the Competition package from December 2004, which cost €5,300 in Germany. [S3, HIGH] [S2, HIGH] BMW's German price lists offer that package for the M3 Coupé only. [S2, HIGH] In the UK the same wheel was sold on the "M3 CS", whose release names "'M3 CSL Design' 19-inch light alloy wheels". [S6, HIGH]

**The CSL's own wheel** is a slightly different 19-inch: wider in front (8.5 inches instead of 8) with a lower offset, on Michelin Cup tyres. [S4, HIGH] BMW's catalogue, as read on our mirror, lists the CSL for Europe only. [S5, MEDIUM]

## Two winter wheels

BMW also listed two wheels for winter tyres only. [S1, HIGH] [S5, MEDIUM]

- **Style 68M (M3 version), 17-inch:** 7.5×17 ET47 in front, 7.5×17 ET25 at the rear. BMW's German price list sold it as a complete winter set at €1,720 from March 2004 to March 2006. [S2, HIGH] BMW's sheet gives the tyres as 225/45 R17 and the price list as 225/40 R17; we can't settle which is right. [S1, HIGH] [S2, HIGH]
- **Style 164M, 18-inch:** 7.5×18 ET41 in front, 7.5×18 ET25 at the rear, on 225/40 R18. [S1, HIGH]

Don't confuse the M3's 17-inch 68M with the Style 68M on other E46s: same design name, but different offsets and different part numbers. [S1, HIGH] [S5, MEDIUM] And some guides call the Style 67 "Style 164". BMW's own sheet lists the 164M as a separate winter wheel. [S1, HIGH]

## The brakes behind them

The standard M3 has 325 mm front discs; the Competition package and the CSL have 345 mm, 20 mm more. Both use 328 mm discs at the rear. [S3, HIGH] [S4, HIGH]

That shapes the wheels. BMW's parts catalogue, as read on our mirror, marks the 17-inch winter wheel "not for the Competition package", and lists no 17-inch wheel for the CSL. The 18-inch and 19-inch wheels are listed over the bigger brakes. [S5, MEDIUM] So in that catalogue, the smallest wheel listed for a Competition or CSL car is 18 inches. [S5, MEDIUM]

## Two rules BMW states outright

- **No snow chains on the summer wheels,** and **only the tyre makes BMW lists** are approved. Both are notes on BMW's own wheel and tyre sheets. [S1, HIGH]
- **The CSL's front wheel is for the CSL only.** BMW's parts catalogue, as read on our mirror, says that for safety reasons the CSL front rim may not be used on the M3 Coupé or Convertible. It doesn't give the reason. [S5, MEDIUM]

## M3 wheels on other E46s, and the reverse

BMW's catalogue, as read on our mirror, lists the M3's rims only for the M3 and the CSL, never for another E46. [S5, MEDIUM] The rear is the problem: the M3's 9×18 ET26 rear sits about 30 mm further out than the 8.5×18 ET50 rear of the other E46s, as simple arithmetic shows. Whether a non-M arch takes that, no source says. The fronts share their width and offset with some non-M 18-inch fronts, but that's a matching number, not proof of fit. [S5, MEDIUM] More on how to judge that in [Will that wheel fit your E46?](/en/blog/will-that-wheel-fit-your-e46)

The other way round, BMW's own M3 sheets list no wheel from the other E46s. [S1, HIGH]

## Buying one today

When the research team checked European BMW parts dealers on 28 October 2026, no complete M3 factory set could be ordered new. [S7, HIGH]
- **18-inch Style 67M:** both front and rear were no longer available.
- **19-inch Style 67M:** the front could be ordered; the rear only at one dealer, while another listed it as no longer available.
- **163M:** only the rear could be ordered.

So for most M3 owners, a set means used wheels. Check that the listing states the size cast into each rim, front and rear, and that the rears really are the wide ones. How to read the rest of a listing is in [Genuine or replica? How to read an E46 wheel listing](/en/blog/e46-wheel-listing-genuine-or-replica).

## Notes on the evidence

**Confidence.** Sizes, offsets, tyres, construction, standard or option status, prices and the brake sizes come from BMW's own wheel and tyre sheets, German price lists and press releases (HIGH). Finishes, option conditions, which cars each rim is listed for, and the CSL front's safety note come from one mirror of BMW's parts catalogue (MEDIUM). Availability is what dealers' pages said on 28 October 2026.

**Not established.** Whether the 163M was ever delivered from the factory on an M3 Convertible: BMW's catalogue lists the rims for it, but the price lists offer the Competition package on the Coupé only. Whether a Competition car could leave the factory on the 19-inch Style 67. Which winter tyre size is right for the 17-inch 68M. Why BMW bars the CSL front wheel from the M3.
