Ask an E46 owner how old their car is and you'll get a year, usually the year it was first registered. For parts, that's often not enough. BMW changed the E46 on specific build months, and a car registered in one year can have been built months earlier, on the other side of a change. [S5] This article puts the changeovers in one place, says where the build month is printed where we know it, and links the articles with the details.

## The changeovers in one place

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| June 2000 | the airbag steering wheels change generation [S2, MEDIUM] | Sedan, Touring, Coupé | [June 2000: the airbag wheels](/en/blog/e46-june-2000-airbag-wheels) |
| June 2000 | the 20-pin diagnostic socket under the bonnet goes, on US cars [S3, HIGH] | US cars | [OBD scanners and the E46](/en/blog/e46-obd-scanners) |
| September 2001 | the facelift, with new headlights and bumpers among other parts [S1, HIGH] | Sedan, Touring | [The 2001 facelift](/en/blog/e46-2001-facelift) |

## Why the month beats the year

- **A year covers both sides** of most of these changes. A 2001 Sedan can be either side of September 2001; a 2003 Coupé either side of March.
- **The registration year can lag the build.** The year most owners know is the year of first registration, and that can be months after the car left the factory. [S5] A Sedan first registered in early 2002 can still be a pre-facelift car.
- **Two dates in the same month.** June 2000 changed both the airbag wheels and, on US cars, the diagnostic socket. A Sedan built in May 2000 and one built in July can need different airbag wheels. [S2, MEDIUM]

This is also why E46BUILD asks about the car itself near a changeover, such as which headlights it has, rather than guessing from the year. [S5]

## Where the build month is printed

- **On US cars,** federal law requires a certification label next to the driver's seat, on the door hinge pillar, the door-latch post or the door edge, showing the "Month and year of manufacture". The rule's own example is "June 2000", or "6/00". [S4] See [49 CFR § 567.4](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2024-title49-vol6/pdf/CFR-2024-title49-vol6-sec567-4.pdf).
- **On European cars,** our research found no BMW document that says where the build date is printed. Ask the seller for the build month, or have it looked up from the car's vehicle identification number (VIN).

## When it matters most

- **Buying used parts for a car built near a changeover:** check the part on your car, not just the year. The part numbers settle it.
- **Buying a car:** a "2002" in a listing doesn't tell you which side of September 2001 it's on. Ask for the build month.
- **In E46BUILD:** if you're close to a changeover, it asks a question about the car instead of guessing. [S5] Answer from what's on the car.
