When people talk about E46 packages, they mean the M Sport package, the Clubsport or the Editions: the ones you can see. BMW's German price lists also sold three packages that mostly made the car nicer to live with. They rarely show in a listing photo, but they explain why one E46 has automatic air conditioning, parking sensors and a better radio, and another doesn't. Here is what each contained, from the lists themselves. [S1, HIGH]

## Comfort package (972)

The Comfort package is in the German lists for the Sedan and Touring from September 2002 to the last ones we have, March 2005, and for the Coupé and Convertible from March 2004 to the last lists in March 2006. [S1, HIGH] Its contents changed with the body and the year. [S1, HIGH]

| List | Bodies | What it included | Price, by model |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| September 2002 | Sedan, Touring | automatic air conditioning; BMW Reverse radio on four-cylinder models or BMW Business on six-cylinders; Park Distance Control; automatically dimming mirror; front armrest; leather steering wheel; velour mats; on-board computer | €1,320 to €1,600 |
| September 2004 | Sedan, Touring | the same, without the leather steering wheel | €1,270 to €1,550 |
| September 2004 | Coupé | automatic air conditioning; BMW Business radio; Park Distance Control; automatically dimming mirror; front armrest; velour mats | €1,300 to €1,450 |
| September 2004 | Convertible | as the Coupé, plus a wind deflector | €1,510 to €1,670 |

From BMW's German price lists. [S1, HIGH] The price depends on the model; the lists print several rows, and some combinations, such as with an Edition or the M Sport package II, change it again.

## Top Comfort package (981)

The Top Comfort package appears in the September 2002 Sedan list. [S1, HIGH] It's the one that changes how the car looks at night:
- xenon headlights with headlight washers, and fog lights;
- seat heating and lumbar support;
- a rain sensor;
- the multi-function steering wheel.

That list prices it at €1,350 to €1,500 by model, and lower in combination with a car phone, phone preparation or the SMG gearbox. [S1, HIGH] We found it in no other list we have, so how long it was offered isn't established.

## Advantage package (730): the Compact's

The Compact got its own package. [S1, HIGH]
- **September 2002:** BMW Reverse radio, air conditioning and star-spoke 45 wheels, at €1,550 to €1,700 by model, or €1,000 with the M Sport package II.
- **September 2004:** BMW Business radio, velour mats, front armrest, leather steering wheel, automatically dimming mirror, fog lights, Park Distance Control, on-board computer and star-spoke 169 wheels, at €900 to €1,950 by model, less again in combination with the M Sport package II or the Edition Lifestyle. The March 2004 list already names the star-spoke 169.

So a 2002 Advantage Compact and a 2004 one are quite different cars inside.

## What it means for a used car

- **The radio:** a Comfort-package car usually has the radio the package named. Which E46 radios exist and what can replace them is in [What's in your dash](/en/blog/e46-infotainment-dash).
- **The headlights:** xenon on a Sedan from around 2002 may have come with Top Comfort rather than on its own. Which unit you have is in [How to tell which E46 headlight you have](/en/blog/e46-headlight-technology-spot-yours).
- **The wheels:** a Compact on star-spoke 45 or 169 may be an Advantage car; the wheels alone don't prove it.
- **The proof:** the car's factory option list shows the package codes, 972, 981 or 730. More questions to ask are in [Ask before you buy](/en/blog/e46-ask-before-you-buy).
