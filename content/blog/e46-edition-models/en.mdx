From 2003 BMW sold the E46 in three Editions: Sport, Exclusive and Lifestyle. They weren't option boxes on top of a car. In BMW's German price lists each Edition is a model with its own price, and each one bundles a different idea of the car. Here is what they added on each body, what they cost, and how to tell one. The picture shows the M Sport package II look that the Edition Sport included; it isn't an Edition car.

## When and on which bodies

BMW's press kit for model year 2003, dated 18 February 2003, introduces "Editionspakete" for the Sedan and Touring. [S2, HIGH] In BMW's German price lists: [S1, HIGH]
- **Sedan and Touring:** all three Editions. They're in the March 2003 price tables [S1, MEDIUM] and in every Sedan and Touring list we have from March 2004 on.
- **Coupé:** all three, from September 2004.
- **Convertible:** the Edition Sport and the Edition Exclusive, from September 2004. No Lifestyle.
- **Compact:** the Edition Lifestyle only, in the March and September 2004 lists.

The codes are 722 for the Lifestyle, 723 for the Exclusive and 724 for the Sport. [S1, HIGH]

## What they cost

Because each Edition is its own model, the price list gives a price per engine. In the September 2004 Sedan list, for the 316i: [S1, HIGH]

| 316i Sedan | Price, Germany, September 2004 |
| --- | --- |
| standard | €24,150 |
| Edition Lifestyle | €25,650 |
| Edition Exclusive | €28,000 |
| Edition Sport | €28,530 |

On the Compact the same list prices the 316ti at €21,800 and the 316ti Edition Lifestyle at €23,400. [S1, HIGH]

## Edition Sport: the M package, and more

BMW's 2003 press kit says the Edition Sport for the Sedan and Touring "entspricht im Wesentlichen dem bekannten M-Sportpaket mit zusätzlichen Sonderausstattungen": essentially the M Sport package, with extra options. [S2, HIGH] The price lists spell it out. [S1, HIGH]
- **The M Sport package II, complete:** the M aerodynamics package, sport suspension, the 68M wheels on the Sedan, Touring and Convertible, sport seats in "Laser" cloth with Alcantara, Shadow Line, the M leather steering wheel and the M door sills.
- **Added on every body:** seat heating, a rain sensor and silver aluminium "cube" trim.
- **Added on the Coupé and Convertible:** xenon headlights, headlight washers and the multi-function steering wheel.
- **Paints** in the Sedan list: three uni colours, alpinweiß III, imolarot II and schwarz II, with the metallics at a reduced surcharge.

What the M Sport package II itself contains, body by body, is in [One option box, four upgrades](/en/blog/e46-m-sport-package-ii).

## Edition Exclusive: leather and light

The Exclusive is the comfort Edition. [S1, HIGH]
- **Sedan and Touring:** Chameleon leather, lumbar support, seat heating, xenon headlights with washers, a rain sensor, the multi-function steering wheel, white indicators and an anthracite headliner. Wheels: Style 136 in 16 inches or star-spoke 137 in 17 inches, depending on the model. A metallic paint from a set list is included.
- **Coupé and Convertible:** Exclusivleder Futura leather, adaptive headlights, xenon with washers, lumbar support, seat heating, a rain sensor, the multi-function steering wheel, poplar-grain trim in smoke grey and star-spoke 171 wheels, 8×17 inches.

BMW's 2003 press kit calls it the "hochwertigere" (higher-grade) Edition, with Chameleon leather seats, and says the colour chiarettorot was reserved for the Edition models. [S2, HIGH]

## Edition Lifestyle: colour and cloth

The Lifestyle is the lighter Edition, and its contents changed over time.
- **Sedan and Touring, 2003:** BMW's press kit describes seats in cloth and leather, "schwarzgrau, mysticblau". [S2, HIGH]
- **Sedan and Touring, September 2004:** plain Jacquard cloth, the sport leather steering wheel, seat heating, a rain sensor, fog lights, white indicators and Style 136 or star-spoke 137 wheels, in three uni colours: japanrot II, schwarz II and alpinweiß III. Scritto cloth/leather was an option within the Edition. [S1, HIGH]
- **Coupé, from September 2004:** Finolino cloth and leather, xenon headlights with washers, seat heating, a rain sensor, poplar-grain trim and star-spoke 170 wheels, 8×17 inches. [S1, HIGH]
- **Compact, 2004:** Scritto cloth and leather, Styling 169 or star-spoke 136 wheels, the sport leather steering wheel with multi-function buttons, white indicators, Titan II trim, seat heating and a rain sensor. [S1, HIGH]

The upholstery names, and which bodies had which, are in [Cloth, leather or Alcantara?](/en/blog/e46-cloth-leather-alcantara)

## Spotting one

- **The upholstery:** Chameleon leather on a Sedan or Touring is the strongest clue; in the September 2004 Sedan list it appears only in the Edition Exclusive. Futura leather and Finolino cloth/leather were also sold as separate options on the Coupé, so on their own they're hints, not proof. [S1, HIGH]
- **The wheels:** star-spoke 137, 170 and 171 are named in the Editions. [S1, HIGH] Whether they were also sold on their own isn't established in our sources.
- **The colour:** chiarettorot was reserved for the Editions in 2003. [S2, HIGH]
- **An Edition Sport** looks like an M Sport package II car; the extras (seat heating, rain sensor, cube trim, on the Coupé and Convertible xenon and the multi-function wheel) are what tell it apart. [S1, HIGH]

Parts swap, so none of these proves an Edition on its own. The car's factory option list should show 722, 723 or 724.

## Notes on the evidence

**Confidence.** The Editions' bodies, codes, prices and contents come from BMW's German price lists (HIGH, Germany only). The March 2003 Sedan and Touring tables are condensed archive copies (MEDIUM). The 2003 introduction, the Lifestyle's first upholstery and chiarettorot come from BMW's own press kit (HIGH). The price lists are private copies, used for facts only.

**Not established.** Which Sedan and Touring models got the 16-inch and which the 17-inch wheel: the lists mark it per model, and we don't restate it here. Whether the Edition wheels were sold separately. When the Sedan and Touring Editions ended: our last Touring list is March 2005. Editions outside Germany.
