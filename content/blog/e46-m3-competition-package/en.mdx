The E46 M3 was already the sharpest car in the range. In late 2004 BMW M offered a package that made the Coupé sharper still, using parts from the M3 CSL. BMW's own press release called it an option "für besonders sportlich orientierte BMW M3-Fahrer", for particularly sporty M3 drivers. [S1, HIGH] Here is what the Competition package contained, from that release and BMW's German price lists. The wheels themselves, and every other M3 wheel, are in [The M3's own wheels](/en/blog/e46-m3-own-wheels).

## When, for which car, at what price

- **From December 2004,** and only when ordering a new car. [S1, HIGH]
- **The M3 Coupé only:** the release names the Coupé, and the German price lists show the package in the Coupé column only. [S1, HIGH] [S2, HIGH]
- **€5,300** in Germany, in the release and in the price lists of March 2005, September 2005 and March 2006. [S1, HIGH] [S2, HIGH]
- **Code 7MA** in the price lists. [S2, HIGH]

## What it contained

From BMW's release and the three price lists, which agree: [S1, HIGH] [S2, HIGH]

| Area | What the package brought |
| --- | --- |
| Wheels and tyres | 19-inch wheels in M3 CSL styling: 8J×19 with 225/40 R 19 in front, 9.5J×19 with 255/35 R 19 at the rear |
| Brakes | the M3 CSL's brakes: drilled compound front discs 20 mm larger, now 345 mm |
| Chassis | optimised suspension, with revised damper and spring settings |
| Steering | a more direct ratio, 14.5:1 instead of 15.4:1 |
| Driving modes | the CSL's M Track Mode, to drive nearer the limit without switching the stability control off completely |
| Steering wheel | an M leather wheel with an Alcantara rim and the M Track Mode button in the right spoke; the multi-function buttons are dropped |
| Interior | Alcantara on the handbrake grip and, with the SMG gearbox, on the gear knob; a new "Alu Tec" trim, exclusive to the package |
| Paint | all M3 Coupé colours, plus a new one: Interlagos Blue metallic |

The price lists add three conditions: the package can't be combined with the eucalyptus wood trim, the multi-function steering wheel or voice control; and with phone preparation, the Professional navigation system has to be ordered too. [S2, HIGH]

## The parts that matter for buyers

- **The wheels and brakes go together.** The package's 345 mm front discs are the CSL's. In BMW's catalogue, as our M3 wheels article read it on one mirror, the 17-inch M3 winter wheel is marked "not for the Competition package", and the smallest wheel listed over the bigger brakes is 18 inches (MEDIUM there). The details are in [The M3's own wheels](/en/blog/e46-m3-own-wheels).
- **The steering wheel is a clue.** An M3 with an Alcantara-rimmed wheel, an M Track Mode button and no multi-function buttons matches the package, but wheels can be swapped. [S1, HIGH]
- **Interlagos Blue** was new with the package. [S1, HIGH] How BMW names this and the other E46 colours is in [Japan Red to Laguna Seca Blue](/en/blog/e46-paint-code).
- **The proof** is 7MA on the car's factory option list.
