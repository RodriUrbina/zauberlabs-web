The Clubsport was BMW's sporty E46 Coupé without the M3's price. BMW's British press release put it "neatly between the 330Ci Sport model and the legendary M3 Coupé". [S1, HIGH] In Germany it's in BMW's price lists from 2002 to the last Coupé list of March 2006, and it didn't stay the same: the wheels, the spoiler and the details changed between price lists. Here is what each version contained, and what we still can't say. The pictures show BMW's M parts on a Coupé; they aren't Clubsport cars.

## Where and on which Coupés

- **UK:** a 330Ci Clubsport Coupé, announced on 22 February 2002, arriving in May, at £31,380. [S1, HIGH]
- **Germany:** the package is in the first German Coupé price list we have, September 2002, for the 320Ci, 325Ci and 330Ci. [S2, MEDIUM] From March 2004 the 330Cd joins them. [S2, MEDIUM] It stays in every list up to the last, March 2006. [S2, HIGH]
- **Never offered** in those German lists on the 316Ci, 318Ci or 320Cd. [S2, HIGH]

The price was €4,950 in the 2002 and March 2004 lists [S2, MEDIUM] and €4,200 from September 2004, when the lists also start printing its option code, 768. [S2, HIGH] BMW's parts catalogue, as read on our mirror, calls it S768A, "Individual series Clubsport". [S3, MEDIUM] Whether code 768 already applied to the 2002 cars isn't established.

The UK release also says 20 kg of insulation was removed "to increase engine sound", and names three colours: Titanium Silver and the Individual colours Estoril Blue and Velvet Blue. [S1, HIGH]

## The wheels

From September 2004 the German lists name the wheel: the **135M**, a staggered 18-inch double spoke. [S2, HIGH] BMW's catalogue, as read on our mirror, lists it at 8×18 ET47 in front, number 36117896470, and 8.5×18 ET50 at the rear, number 36117896490. [S3, MEDIUM] As an alternative, those lists offer the **Style 71** 18-inch composite wheel, option 795, at €750, and only with the Clubsport. [S2, HIGH] The March 2004 list already offers that Style 71 alternative. [S2, MEDIUM]

**The early cars are the open question.** The UK release says "exclusive 18 inch double spoke" and the 2002 German list says 18-inch M light-alloy wheels, but neither names the style. [S1, HIGH] [S2, MEDIUM] The 135M was on sale as a separate option in the March 2004 Coupé list, so it existed then. [S2, MEDIUM] Our research rates the 135M as the best-supported guess for the early cars too, but no BMW document we hold says so.

## The rear spoiler: three names

The spoiler is where the package changed most visibly. BMW's German lists call it an M rear spoiler in 2002, a free-standing rear wing in March 2004, and from September 2004 a spoiler lip as standard, with the wing as a separate option, number 754, only with the Clubsport. [S2, MEDIUM] [S2, MEDIUM] [S2, HIGH] So "the Clubsport spoiler" can mean a lip or a wing, depending on the year. Which part BMW's catalogue holds, and how to buy it, is in [The Coupé's rear M spoiler lip](/en/blog/e46-coupe-rear-m-spoiler-lip).

## The seats: not "Laser"

The Clubsport's sport seats are not the M Sport package's "Laser" cloth with Alcantara. The German lists describe Alcantara with M texture and Montana leather, in anthracite and black. [S2, HIGH] In BMW's catalogue, as read on our mirror, that's upholstery code X1AT, with its own seat cover, number 52107895592, and backrest cover, number 52107895594. [S3, MEDIUM] So a listing that says "Clubsport seats" should show leather and Alcantara, not cloth. How the other E46 upholsteries are named is in [Cloth, leather or Alcantara?](/en/blog/e46-cloth-leather-alcantara)

## The early front corners

Only the 2002 and March 2004 German lists name aero corners on the front apron in satin black. [S2, MEDIUM] BMW's catalogue, as read on our mirror, ties a pair of "Clubsport" lateral trim panels to the package; the originals are listed as discontinued, and the pair is now sold primed, numbers 51718067041 (left) and 51718067042 (right). [S3, MEDIUM]

## Telling a real one

- **The door sills** say "Clubsport" in every version. [S1, HIGH] [S2, HIGH] Sills can be swapped, so they're a hint, not proof.
- **The option code** in BMW's catalogue is S768A. [S3, MEDIUM] That's the code to look for in the car's factory option list.
- **The model:** in Germany, a 316Ci, 318Ci or 320Cd was never offered with it. [S2, HIGH]
- **The seats** should be Alcantara with M texture and Montana leather, as above. [S2, HIGH]

The parts themselves fit Coupés BMW never sold as a Clubsport: the 135M, for one, is in BMW's catalogue, as read on our mirror, for every European Coupé. [S3, MEDIUM] A car can wear the look without having been one.

## Notes on the evidence

**Confidence.** The German contents from September 2004 come from six BMW price lists that agree (HIGH). The 2002 and March 2004 contents rest on one list each (MEDIUM). The UK version comes from BMW's own British press release (HIGH, UK only). Part numbers, the option code and the upholstery code come from one mirror of BMW's parts catalogue (MEDIUM). The price lists are private copies, used for facts only.

**Not established.** Which wheel the 2002 to 2004 cars had. Whether the one spoiler in BMW's Coupé catalogue group is the lip or the free-standing wing; the wing has no part number in our evidence. Whether code 768 already applied in 2002. Why the March 2003 Coupé list, a condensed table, shows no Clubsport row.
