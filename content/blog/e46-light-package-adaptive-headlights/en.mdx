The facelift E46 Coupé and Convertible could have headlights that turn into the bend as you steer. BMW's German price lists sold them in two ways: on their own, with conditions attached, or as part of a Light package that bundled everything they needed. Here is what the package contained, what each part cost on its own, and why the conditions matter today. What an adaptive headlight is, and why swapping one is not simple, is in [The E46 headlight that works, until it doesn't](/en/blog/e46-adaptive-headlights-not-a-simple-swap).

## The Light package (7RM)

From March 2004, BMW's German price lists for the Coupé and Convertible offer a Light package, code 7RM. [S1, HIGH] In the September 2004 Coupé list it includes: [S1, HIGH]
- **xenon headlights**;
- **headlight washers**;
- a **rain sensor**;
- **adaptive headlights**, the ones that turn.

It costs €910 in the March 2004, September 2004 and March 2006 Coupé lists. [S1, HIGH] With the Edition Lifestyle or Sport it costs €400, and the Edition Exclusive already contains xenon and the adaptive headlights. [S1, HIGH] What the Editions contained is in [Edition Sport, Exclusive, Lifestyle](/en/blog/e46-edition-models).

## The same parts on their own

In the September 2004 Coupé list, each part also has its own price: [S1, HIGH]

| Option | Code | Price, September 2004 | Conditions in the list |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Xenon light, low and high beam | 522 | €790 | |
| Headlight washers | 502 | €260 | included with xenon |
| Rain sensor and automatic headlight control | 521 | €110 | |
| Adaptive headlights | 524 | €400 | only with the rain sensor and xenon; includes headlight bezels in titanium |

From BMW's German price list for the Coupé, September 2004. [S1, HIGH]

So the three options the adaptive light needed, xenon, the rain sensor and the adaptive light itself, came to €1,300 on their own, against €910 as the package: a saving of €390, by simple arithmetic.

**Before the package.** The March 2003 Coupé and Convertible price tables already list the adaptive headlights at €400, only with the rain sensor and xenon, and xenon at €790 including the washers. [S1, MEDIUM] So the adaptive light was already offered in March 2003, the month of the Coupé and Convertible facelift; the package that bundled it came a year later.

## Why the conditions matter now

The list's condition, adaptive only with xenon and the rain sensor, is the factory version of what our [adaptive-headlights article](/en/blog/e46-adaptive-headlights-not-a-simple-swap) finds in the catalogue: the adaptive units are bi-xenon, and they need the car's own adaptive system to work. A car with the Light package had all of it from the factory. A car with xenon alone didn't have the adaptive system, and as that article explains, adaptive units fitted to such a car light but don't turn, and their levelling doesn't work (MEDIUM and LOW there).

For a used Coupé or Convertible:
- **7RM or 524** on the factory option list means the car left the factory with adaptive headlights.
- **The titanium headlight bezels** come with the adaptive light in the list; they're a hint, not proof, since headlights can be swapped. [S1, HIGH]
- **The test** from the adaptive article still applies: do the low beams turn when you steer at night?
