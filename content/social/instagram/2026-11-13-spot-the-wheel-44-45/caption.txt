Seven spokes. Which inch?
Style 44 and Style 45 are easy to mix up: seven spokes each, both from February 1998. The 44 is 17-inch and 8J wide, the 45 is 16-inch and 7J, both with a 47 mm offset. With tyres on, a photo won't settle it: read the size cast into the back of the rim.
And the European catalogue lists the Style 45 for no 330 at all.
