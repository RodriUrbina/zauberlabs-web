Tick box 338.
What your E46 could have had: the M Sport package II.
On 14 May 2001 BMW launched the new M Sport package: M aerodynamics kit, 17-inch M double-spoke wheels on mixed tyres, sport suspension, the M Sport leather steering wheel, and sport seats in "Laser" cloth with Alcantara.
BMW's German price lists call it code 338. The wheel: 68M on the Sedan, Touring, Coupé and Convertible, 97M on the Compact; from March 2004 the four larger bodies could have the 135M instead.
