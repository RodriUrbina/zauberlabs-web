M3. Its own wheels.
According to BMW's own wheel and tyre sheets, the 18-inch Style 67M is standard on the M3 Coupé and Convertible: 8×18 ET47 in front, 9×18 ET26 at the rear. The 19-inch version, forged and polished, was factory option 792.
And behind them: the standard M3 has 325 mm front discs, the Competition package and the CSL 345 mm.
