Package 7RM.
Per BMW's German price lists, from March 2004 the Coupé and Convertible could have the Light package: xenon, headlight washers, a rain sensor and adaptive headlights, for €910. On their own, xenon, the rain sensor and the adaptive light added up to €1,300 in September 2004.
The list offers adaptive headlights only with xenon and the rain sensor. That's why an adaptive unit isn't a plug-in for a car with xenon alone.
