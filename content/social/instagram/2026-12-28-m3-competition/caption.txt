Competition. Since December 2004.
Per BMW's press release of November 2004 and the German price lists, from December 2004 the M3 Coupé could have the Competition package, code 7MA, for €5,300: 19-inch wheels in CSL styling, the CSL's brakes with 345 mm front discs, optimised suspension, quicker steering at 14.5:1 instead of 15.4:1, and M Track Mode.
Plus a steering wheel with an Alcantara rim and no multi-function buttons, "Alu Tec" trim and a new colour: Interlagos Blue.
