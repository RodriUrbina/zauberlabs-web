June 2000. Mind the airbag.
Per BMW's parts catalogue, the Sedan, Touring and Coupé airbag wheels change generation in June/July 2000; the Convertible, Compact and M3 have the "Smart" wheels from the start.
The part numbers tell the generations apart, for example 32346753945 (older four-spoke wheel) and 32346753947 (Smart). What counts is your car's build month, not its first registration.
