Mind the month.
A Sedan built in August 2001 and one built in September 2001 take different headlights and bumpers in BMW's parts catalogue: the facelift parts start in September 2001. The Coupé and Convertible follow in March 2003, and the airbag steering wheels had already changed in June/July 2000.
The year on the papers is usually the year of first registration, and that can lag the build by months. Ask for the build month.
