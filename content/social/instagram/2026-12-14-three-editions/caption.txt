Three Editions. One idea each.
From 2003 BMW sold the E46 as the Edition Sport, Exclusive and Lifestyle, each one its own model with its own price in the German price lists. In September 2004 a 316i Sedan cost €24,150, as an Edition Lifestyle €25,650, as an Exclusive €28,000 and as a Sport €28,530.
The Sport is, in BMW's 2003 press kit, "essentially" the M Sport package with extra options; the lists name seat heating, a rain sensor and cube trim. The Exclusive brings leather, xenon and comfort, the Lifestyle colour and cloth.
