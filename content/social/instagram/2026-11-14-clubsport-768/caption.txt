Option 768.
The Clubsport came to the UK first: in February 2002 BMW announced the 330Ci Clubsport Coupé, with a rear M spoiler, sports suspension settings, 18-inch double-spoke wheels and leather/Alcantara sports seats, at £31,380.
In Germany the Clubsport appears from the September 2004 price list as option 768 on most Coupé models, at €4,200, and names the 135M as its wheel.
