Facelift. In stages.
According to BMW's parts catalogue, the Sedan and Touring changed in September 2001, the Coupé and Convertible in March 2003. For the Compact, the catalogue, as we read it on one mirror, shows no date split for its headlights, but new rear lights in March 2003.
BMW's British press kit wouldn't even call it a facelift: the 1998 3 Series was "hardly old enough to have suffered from facial ageing".
