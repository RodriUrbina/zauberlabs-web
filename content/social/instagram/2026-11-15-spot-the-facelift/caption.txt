Spot the facelift.
On the Sedan and Touring, BMW draws the line at September 2001: every headlight number before it is replaced after it, bi-xenon replaces xenon, and the standard bumpers change too. The Coupé and Convertible get their visible facelift in March 2003.
The trap: a car built in 2001 can't be placed on either side of the line from its year alone.
