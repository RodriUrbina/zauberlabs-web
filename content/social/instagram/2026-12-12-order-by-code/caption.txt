Order by code.
One colour, two names: Japan Red is japanrot II in BMW's German price lists, and BMW's German press wrote Laguna Seca Blue as Laguna-seca Blau. Some German names carry a numeral, and the lists don't say what it means.
BMW's parts catalogue, as read on our mirror, sells painted parts by colour code, for example the Coupé's rear spoiler "lackiert, CODE - UNI/MET."
Where the code sticker sits isn't in any BMW document we hold; owners on forums point to a strut tower under the bonnet.
